I’m Ndikora. I’ve been arranging flowers in Kampala for two years, and I still believe the flower is never the real gift.

My friends call me a lover boy, and I don’t mind it. To me, flowers are love, plain and simple. I’ve had people tell me, “don’t buy me flowers, give me money instead,” and it still pricks me a little, because that misses the point entirely. We give flowers knowing they will die. That is what makes it the best act of love there is. A flower can’t be saved, resold or kept for later. It only says one thing: I thought of you when I saw this bouquet, and I wanted you to have it. Even at funerals, when we send someone away, we give them something pretty to carry. I started Eden of Kora two years ago to hold onto that thought, one bouquet at a time.
