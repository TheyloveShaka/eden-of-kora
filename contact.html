Frequently Asked

What areas do you deliver to, and how much is delivery?

I deliver across Kampala. The fee depends on your area and is confirmed on WhatsApp before you pay, so you always know the full cost before you commit.

What time do I need to order for same-day delivery?

Message me on WhatsApp and I’ll confirm the next available delivery slot with you.

How and when do I pay?

Payment comes before delivery, by MTN MoMo, Airtel Money, bank transfer or Visa/Mastercard. I don’t accept cash on delivery.

How do I keep my flowers fresh?

Keep them in cool water, out of direct sun. I don’t add flower food. With that care, most varieties last 10 to 14 days.

Can I request a custom or sympathy arrangement?

Yes. Message me your colours, size, budget and the occasion, birthday or sympathy, and I’ll put something together for you.

What happens if my order arrives damaged or wrong?

Message me a photo on WhatsApp as soon as it arrives. If it’s damaged or wrong, I’ll replace it.
