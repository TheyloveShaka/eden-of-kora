Corporate FAQ

Is there a minimum order for corporate accounts?

There’s no fixed minimum. Tell me the space and how often you’d like flowers, and I’ll suggest what fits your budget.

How is the price worked out?

Price depends on quantity, size, how often you order and your delivery area. I send a quote once I know those details.

When do we need to pay?

Payment is due before each delivery, by MTN MoMo, Airtel Money, bank transfer or card.

Can deliveries repeat on a schedule?

Yes. Tell me how often you’d like fresh flowers, weekly or monthly, and I’ll build a recurring delivery around it.
